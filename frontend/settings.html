About BreadWinner

v0.1 — prototype · built for the Congressional App Challenge.

BreadWinner helps track gluten-free spending and potential tax deductions. Receipt scanning and product matching are not yet connected in this prototype. BreadWinner never files taxes or gives tax advice.

Reset settings to defaults?

Theme, accent color, notifications, currency, date format, and reduce-motion go back to their defaults. Your name, email, and diagnosis info are not affected.

Clear all local data?

This removes your name, email, diagnosis info, avatar color, and preferences from this device. Your dashboard will reset to its default state. This can't be undone.
