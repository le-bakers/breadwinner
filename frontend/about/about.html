Hey, it's us!

We are the team behind BreadWinner. We are passionate about helping households manage their gluten-free grocery costs more effectively.

Our story

We started with a problem that deserved more care.

Our project originated when an individual who is gluten-intolerant found it difficult to manage their grocery expenses. They wanted a solution that would make it easier to track and organize their costs, so here we are: BreadWinner.

What we bring

A variety of perspectives, one shared purpose.

We take great care in the work we do and the people we serve. Many people spend lots of time, money, and energy tallying up the receipts, trying to track what items could or couldn't be tax deducted. We're here to change that.
