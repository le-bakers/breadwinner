Who operates this project

BreadWinner is a student-built prototype for the Congressional App Challenge, intended for adults in the United States. It is not an incorporated organization, medical provider, tax preparer, or government service. This notice describes the software in this release. Contact the student developers at support.breadwinner@gmail.com.

Before tracking receipts, you must separately accept our Terms and consent to the receipt processing described in the Consumer Health Data Privacy Policy. You can browse public pages without creating an account.

Information collected and purposes

  • Account: your name, email, authentication identifiers, and provider information, to create and secure your account. Supabase handles email/password authentication; Google handles Google sign-in if you select it. We do not store plaintext passwords. We record adult confirmation and versions and timestamps of accepted notices, rather than your birth date.
  • Receipts: photos, store, date, items, prices, quantities, comparisons, and gluten-free classifications you submit or confirm, to organize receipts and calculate extra-cost estimates. Purchases and classifications may reveal health information.
  • Location: the ZIP code you enter for nearby store prices. We do not request GPS location.
  • Browser storage: authentication, account display details, optional profile photo, ZIP code, preferences, and, in demo mode, receipts. See Cookies & Storage.
  • Technical information: our hosting and authentication services may process IP addresses, request metadata, browser information, and security logs to deliver and protect the site. We keep bounded request counters in server memory for abuse prevention.
  • Support: messages you choose to email us, used to answer your request. The contact form opens an email draft; it does not silently send a message.

Receipt processing and automated results

Receipt OCR in this release runs on the BreadWinner backend using local OCR software. The backend decodes approved image formats and removes embedded metadata before OCR. It does not intentionally save uploaded original photos to its filesystem or send them to a cloud AI provider in the default configuration. It returns extracted items to your browser; the browser saves a reduced receipt image and the reviewed records to your account.

Automated readings and gluten-free guesses can be wrong. Review and correct results. They are not a diagnosis, food-safety decision, verified overcharge, or tax eligibility determination. Crop or cover loyalty numbers, payment details, addresses, and other unnecessary information before uploading.

Your privacy choices and requests

In Settings you can export account receipts and local profile/preferences as JSON, clear local data, withdraw receipt-processing consent, or delete your account and its linked receipts. Clearing local data does not delete cloud records. Withdrawal stops subsequent account receipt access and processing until you consent again; it does not automatically delete saved receipts. Export first, or email us for assistance if you no longer wish to consent.

Email support.breadwinner@gmail.com to request access, correction, deletion, a copy, withdrawal, or an appeal of a denied request. State your request and the account email; do not send passwords, identity documents, diagnoses, or full receipts. We may verify account control and ask only for information needed to fulfill the request. Authorized agents may contact us with authorization. Where applicable law grants these rights, we will respond within its required time and explain any permitted refusal or extension. You may also complain to your state Attorney General or privacy regulator. We do not penalize you for exercising applicable privacy rights.

We do not sell personal or consumer health data, share it for cross-context behavioral advertising, run targeted advertising, or use it for marketing health profiles. No sale or advertising-sharing workflow is enabled, including when a browser sends Global Privacy Control or Do Not Track. This release contains no product analytics, ad pixels, or session replay.

Retention and deletion

Account records, receipt records and reduced photos remain in the active database until you delete them or your account. The account deletion function removes the account and cascades to its receipt and consent rows. Authentication sessions and related provider records follow the provider lifecycle; exported files, copies on other devices, messages, infrastructure logs and backups are not erased by clearing browser data. Provider backups and logs may remain for their configured retention periods; contact us for help with a provider-specific deletion request. We have not independently verified a fixed backup-erasure deadline.

Original uploads are used during a scan and are not intentionally retained by the backend after processing. Necessary security counters are held in bounded process memory. Local storage lasts until you clear it; session storage normally ends when the tab closes. Keep your own backups of needed records because this prototype may change or be discontinued.

Security and children

The production deployment uses HTTPS. The code includes authenticated API access, upload and rate limits, image validation, restrictive browser policies, and database rules intended to limit each account to its own records. These controls require the documented deployment and database configuration. No service or shared browser is completely secure, and we do not claim audited, certified, or HIPAA-compliant operation. We will assess and address security incidents and provide legally required notifications.

This service is not intended for people under 18 or below their local age of majority. We do not knowingly solicit their personal information. Contact us if a child has submitted personal information so we can investigate and arrange deletion. Do not upload someone else’s receipts without appropriate authority.
