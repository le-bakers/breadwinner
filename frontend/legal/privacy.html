01Overview

This policy explains what BreadWinner ("we," "us," or "our") collects and what happens to it. Gluten-free grocery receipts can hint that someone has celiac disease, so we treat all of your data as sensitive and collect only what the app needs.

We do not sell your data, show ads, use tracking or analytics tools, or let other users see your receipts. We never ask for your diagnosis or medical records.

04Receipt Reading by Google Gemini

When you scan a receipt, the photo is sent to Google's Gemini AI to read it. Before sending, we shrink the photo and remove hidden details such as where it was taken. Our scanner server does not keep the photo.

BreadWinner uses Google's free Gemini service. Under Google's terms for that service, Google may use what we send to improve its products, and human reviewers at Google may read it. If you are not comfortable with that for a receipt, use "Enter manually" instead of scanning it.

05How Your Data Is Protected

Database rules let only your account read or change your receipts. Receipt photos sit in private storage and open through links that expire after 6 hours. Data is encrypted while it travels (HTTPS) and while it is stored.

The few BreadWinner team members who run the database can technically reach it; we only look at account data when needed to fix a problem. No system is perfectly secure. If a breach affects your data, we will tell you as the law requires.

06Your Choices

  • Correct any receipt, item, or price in the app.
  • Download everything in Settings → Data & privacy, or export tax summaries from Receipt History.
  • Delete a single receipt (its photo goes with it) or your whole account in Settings → Data & privacy.
  • Clear this device to remove BreadWinner data saved in your browser.

For anything else, email us.

07Keeping & Deleting Data

We keep your data until you delete it. Deleting a receipt or your account removes it from our database and photo storage right away. Copies in our providers' technical logs or backups may last a short time longer, until those expire on the providers' own schedules.

08Children

BreadWinner is only for people 18 and older, and we check age at sign-up. If we learn that someone under 18 has an account, we will delete it.
