Access to the prototype

BreadWinner is a student-built Congressional App Challenge prototype. We aim to make its features usable with keyboard navigation, clear labels, visible focus, screen-reader announcements and reduced-motion preferences. We have not completed an independent accessibility audit and do not claim certified conformance.

Assistance and feedback

If an accessibility barrier prevents you from using a feature or exercising a privacy right, email support.breadwinner@gmail.com. Describe the affected page and, if helpful, your browser and assistive technology. Do not send private receipt or medical details. We will work with you on an accessible alternative for information, export or deletion requests.

You can enable reduced motion in Settings or use your device preference. Receipt scanning results can be reviewed and corrected; a photo upload can be used when camera access is unavailable. Updated October 22, 2026.
