02What BreadWinner Does

You photograph, upload, or type in grocery receipts. BreadWinner reads them, guesses which items are gluten-free, and helps you compare each one with the price of a regular (non-gluten-free) version. It keeps a running total of that extra cost, the amount that may count toward the IRS medical-expense deduction, so you can give organized records to a tax professional or use them yourself.

BreadWinner is free. It does not file taxes, and it is not a medical, legal, or tax advice service.

06Not Tax Advice

Every dollar total in BreadWinner is an organized estimate, not a filed or guaranteed number. Confirm it with a tax professional before you file.

Under IRS rules (Publication 502), the extra cost of gluten-free food can count as a medical expense only if a doctor has diagnosed you with celiac disease, and it lowers your taxes only if you itemize deductions on Schedule A and your total medical expenses are more than 7.5% of your adjusted gross income. The "potential deduction" BreadWinner shows is that extra cost, not money you will get back.

You are responsible for what you file and for keeping your receipts and your doctor's written diagnosis.

07Accuracy

BreadWinner uses automated tools, including AI receipt reading, gluten-free guessing, and store price lookups, that can be wrong. Mistakes are more likely with faded receipts, store abbreviations, or products sold under the same name in gluten-free and regular versions. Regular-version prices come from the Kroger-family store nearest your zip code or from prices you type in, and may differ from what you would pay elsewhere.

Check each item before relying on it. You can correct any item, price, or comparison in the app.
