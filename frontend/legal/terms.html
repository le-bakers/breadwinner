Receipts, health and tax limitations

The service reads photos, guesses which items are specialty gluten-free products, suggests comparisons and calculates extra-cost estimates. Review every item, price, quantity, package size and comparison. Results may be incomplete or wrong; retain your originals. A gluten-free label in this app does not establish food safety or suitability for a medical condition. A price difference does not prove a store overcharged you.

Extra-cost totals are organizational estimates, not a tax deduction, tax return, refund guarantee, or medical advice. Tax eligibility depends on facts and applicable rules. Seek qualified advice before filing. For US federal guidance, consult IRS Publication 502. We do not determine medical necessity, reimbursement, itemization, or adjusted-gross-income limits, and we do not file returns.
