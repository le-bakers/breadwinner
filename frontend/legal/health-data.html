Scope and categories

BreadWinner is a student-built Congressional App Challenge prototype for US adults. This separate policy explains consumer health data practices, including rights available where applicable state laws cover the project. It supplements the Privacy Policy.

We collect grocery receipt images, purchases, gluten-free classifications, comparisons and linked account identifiers from you. Those records can reveal or imply celiac disease, gluten sensitivity, dietary needs or other health circumstances, even when you have not supplied a diagnosis. Sources are your uploads, entries and corrections, OCR-derived receipt information, and product comparisons from Kroger. We do not request diagnoses, medical records, GPS location, or access to a health app.

Purposes and collection choices

We process this information to read and organize receipts, flag possible gluten-free items, compare product prices and calculate extra-cost estimates that you review. A separate, unchecked choice asks for your consent before receipt tracking. If you decline, public information remains available, while account receipt features are unavailable. We do not collect or use consumer health data for advertising or sell it.

Photo processing runs on our backend with local OCR. Receipt images are not sent to a cloud AI service in this release’s default configuration. Do not upload personal medical records or unnecessary loyalty, payment, address or identity details.

Recipients and sharing

Supabase processes linked account, receipt and consent records as the database/authentication provider. Render processes uploaded receipt images in backend memory as the hosting provider. Authorized student maintainers may access data as needed for operation, security and support. Kroger receives ZIP codes and product search terms when you request comparisons. Website hosting providers process delivery and security metadata. The Privacy Policy lists these providers and purposes.

We do not sell consumer health data, share it with advertisers, or enable sharing with independent third parties for their own health profiling. We do not geofence healthcare facilities. We use providers to deliver the features you request, and may make disclosures required by law. A new optional disclosure or use requiring consent must be separately explained and offered before it begins; accepting the Terms does not authorize it.

Access, withdrawal, deletion and appeals

Use Settings to export records, withdraw receipt consent, or delete your account. Withdrawal stops future receipt-feature access until you consent again; existing records remain until deleted. Export before withdrawal, or ask us for access without renewed consent. Account deletion removes active receipt and consent rows; provider backups and infrastructure logs follow their configured retention, and we will coordinate any further deletion duties that apply.

To confirm whether we process your consumer health data, request access or deletion, request information about recipients, withdraw consent, or appeal a decision, email support.breadwinner@gmail.com. Use the account email if possible. We verify account control without requesting unnecessary health or identity information. We will follow the deadlines, deletion scope and appeal duties of applicable law. You may contact your state Attorney General or privacy regulator about unresolved concerns, including the Washington Attorney General where relevant.

Operator and changes

The student developers operating BreadWinner can be reached at support.breadwinner@gmail.com. Policy version: October 22, 2026. Material changes require an updated notice and any renewed consent required before new processing.
