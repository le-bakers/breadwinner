What this release uses

BreadWinner does not use advertising cookies, product analytics, tracking pixels, or session replay. Its own application code uses localStorage and sessionStorage for requested account and receipt features and preferences. These technologies can be subject to cookie and device-storage rules even when they are not HTTP cookies. Authentication or hosting providers may also use necessary cookies on their own endpoints.

There are no optional advertising or analytics categories to accept in this release. A generic “accept all cookies” banner would not describe the current site. If optional tracking is added, it must remain off until any required, informed choice is made, with an equally accessible rejection and withdrawal option.

Your controls

Settings → Clear Data removes BreadWinner local and session storage on this browser, including local authentication. It does not delete cloud receipts or provider sessions on other devices. Settings → Delete Account removes the active cloud account and linked records. Browser settings can also remove storage or block it; blocking required storage may break sign-in and saving.

We do not sell data or use cross-site advertising sharing, irrespective of Global Privacy Control or Do Not Track. This release serves fonts and the authentication library with its own static assets. Selecting Google sign-in or following an external retailer or social link opens that provider’s service under its own privacy and cookie practices. Contact support.breadwinner@gmail.com for help.
