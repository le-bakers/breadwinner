Reset settings to defaults?

Theme, date format, and motion preferences return to their defaults. Your profile and receipts are kept.

Clear data from this device?

This removes BreadWinner profile details, preferences, and any local receipts from this browser, then signs you out. It does not delete your cloud account or its receipts. Local data cannot be recovered unless you exported it.

Delete your account?

This permanently deletes your account and its saved receipts, clears BreadWinner data from this browser, and signs you out. This cannot be undone. Download your data before continuing if you need a copy.
